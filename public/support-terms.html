Support

Email webmaster@mikegtn.net for help, bug reports, accessibility issues or questions about these terms.

Include the tool or page involved, the time of the problem, the station and service date, and any error message. A screenshot or generated map link can help. Do not send passwords, API keys, payment details or other unnecessary personal information.

Support is provided on a best-effort basis, with no guaranteed response time. For tickets, refunds, accessibility assistance or urgent travel advice, contact the relevant train operator. TrainBrain cannot manage bookings or railway operations.
